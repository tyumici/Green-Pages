## COMMANDS

Commands are replied to by my bot account LCOLINQ

### !joel

Plays a Joel fact audio clip at random. (if you are interested in adding to this project, feel free to reach out!)

<hr>

### !game

Links out to the [treeshaker](https://tree.cedrs.org) game I've made. You get 10 free turns on sign-up, more turns can be redeemed by Channel Points.

<hr>

### !followage

Returns the follow age of your account

<hr>

### !plate
***Aliases:*** !lp

Generate a license plate from one of 65 regions at random. (50 US States + DC + PR + 13 CA provinces/territories)

<hr>

### !collection

Returns a URL to view your license plate collection.

<hr>

### !today

Plays audio prompting me to explain what we're working on today.

<hr>

### !help

Responds with a link to this doc file

<hr>

### !commands

Returns back all the available commands (even ones not mentioned here)

<hr>
