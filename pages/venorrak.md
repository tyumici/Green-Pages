### !joelcount
***Aliases:*** !jcount, !jc

***Optional Parameters:*** username

Displays the number of times a user has said "Joel" or one of its variants in the chat of any of the channnels.

<hr>


### !joelcountchannel
***Aliases:*** !jcountchannel, !jcc

***Optional Parameters:*** channel_name

Displays the number of times "Joel" or one of its variants has been said in a specific channel.

<hr>

### !joelcountstream
***Aliases:*** !jcountstream, !jcs

Displays the number of times "Joel" or one of its variants has been said in the current channel today.

<hr>

### !joeltop
***Aliases:*** !jtop, !jt

Displays the top 5 users who have said "Joel" or one of its variants the most across all channels.

<hr>

### !joeltopchannel
***Aliases:*** !jtopchannel, !jtc

Displays the top 5 channels where "Joel" or one of its variants has been said the most.

<hr>

### !jcp

Display the percentage value of the Joel Consciousness Project(JCP) at this moment.

<hr>

### !joeldrawer
***Aliases:*** !jdraw, !jd

Gives the user a random "Joel" variant associated with a number.
